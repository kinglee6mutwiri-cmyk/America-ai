AI Ethics in Kenya: How to Use AI Responsibly 2026

With great power comes great responsibility. Here's how Kenyans should use AI.

1. Don't Cheat in School

Use AI to learn, not to copy. Teachers can detect AI writing. Write in your own words.

2. Respect People's Privacy

Don't use AI to create fake photos or videos of people without permission. It's illegal.

3. Avoid Bias

AI sometimes gives wrong info about Africans. Always check facts and add Kenyan context.

4. Give Credit

If you use AI to help write a business proposal or essay, say "I used AI to help with this".

5. Help Others Learn

Teach your friends, parents, and teachers how to use AI safely. Don't gatekeep.

Kenya's AI Rules

The government is working on AI policies. For now: be honest, be safe, be helpful.

Good vs Bad Use

Good: AI to study, start business, translate
Bad: AI for scams, cheating, spreading fake news

Golden Rule: Use AI in a way you'd be proud to explain to your grandmother.
